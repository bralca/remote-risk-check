The product judgment

Automation should remove work—not accountability.

The system keeps facts, recommendations, and decisions separate. That makes it faster for operators and defensible for Risk, Treasury, Legal, and customers.

01

Rules decide the status

Structured, versioned policy determines green, yellow, or red. Operations can understand why every rule fired.

02

AI makes evidence usable

The investigator brief summarizes signals, cites its inputs, surfaces uncertainty, and asks for what is missing.

03

People own the action

Clear, reserve, and hold actions require explicit human approval and leave a readable audit trail.

Why this matters to Remote

Trust is part of the product—and the growth engine.

Proof under the surface

Simple to review. Serious enough to inspect.

What is actually working?+

Three typed synthetic cases run through a deterministic, tested policy engine. Changing policy updates the assessment; human approval creates an in-session audit record.

What does AI do here?+

Precomputed briefs turn structured evidence into a concise investigation narrative. They cite source fields, expose uncertainty, and cannot execute an action.

Is it compatible with Remote?+

A server-side adapter verifies Remote-shaped webhook signatures, timestamps, supported event types, and replay attempts. No credentials are needed for this public demo.
