# Product Note: Risk decisions should be faster to make and easier to defend

Remote’s global employment infrastructure creates a difficult product balance: prevent fraud, credit loss, and compliance failures without delaying legitimate employers and their new hires. Manual review cannot grow proportionally with payment and onboarding volume.

The prototype starts with one narrow workflow: employer eligibility during EOR onboarding. The operator sees the evidence, deterministic policy result, evidence-grounded AI summary, customer consequence, and final human action in one place.

The design separates responsibilities:

1. **Rules determine status.** They are consistent, testable, and versionable.
2. **AI explains evidence.** It summarizes, cites inputs, identifies missing information, and exposes uncertainty.
3. **Humans approve action.** Consequential decisions remain accountable and auditable.

A reserve is treated as a reversible safeguard, not a fraud judgment. When verification is complete but financial exposure is high relative to observable history, a reserve can protect payroll obligations while allowing the customer to continue.

The primary metrics would be false-positive rate, risk recall, straight-through processing, manual review rate, protected exposure, reserve coverage, and time to a defensible decision. This public prototype does not invent targets because credible targets require Remote’s labeled outcomes, operating costs, and loss data.

Next, I would validate the workflow with Risk Operations and Treasury, instrument review reasons, and backtest policy changes on labeled historical cases before expanding into payment anomalies, freezes, disputes, collections, and enforcement.

All companies, inputs, thresholds, reserve amounts, and outputs are fictional. This project is independent and based only on Remote’s public materials.
